# Flexible Scheduling Grid Without Regime Lockout

To maximize operational agility across both executive evening/weekend programs ("Temps Aménagé") and standard daytime programs ("Formation Initiale"), we decided not to restrict or hard-block timetable slots by modality. The FullCalendar interface supports unrestricted drag-and-drop across the full operational hours (08:00–22:00, 7 days a week), treating program modality as informative metadata rather than a rigid scheduling wall.
